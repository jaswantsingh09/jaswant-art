    Studio

    The other half of the work. Paintings that took longer than any migration, poems written between deploys, and notes on what I notice.

    Garbage Collection

    I keep old griefs in memory
    long after their references die —
    some part of me still marks them reachable,
    afraid of the silence they’d free.

    Attention

    The model weighs each word against the rest;
    so do we, at midnight, replaying a sentence
    someone said in passing — softmax over memory,
    one moment scored above a thousand days.

    Monsoon, Debugged

    The rain arrives like a hotfix at 2 a.m. —
    unplanned, loud, and somehow
    exactly what the parched system needed.

    Notes

    • Good code and good brushwork share a rule: every stroke should look inevitable.
    • A system diagram is a still life of pressure.
    • The best abstractions, like the best poems, are judged by what they leave out.
